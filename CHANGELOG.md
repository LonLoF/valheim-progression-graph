# Changelog

How the 2026-10-28 v9 dataset was put together. The site shows the current entries only.

## 2026-10-28 v9

1,167 entries, including 69 structures (dungeons, villages, ruins and fortresses).

### Where an entry is listed

An entry sits in the earliest biome where it can be obtained: gathered or found, dropped by a creature, taken from a chest, bought from a trader after any unlock, or crafted or built once every ingredient and the station at the required level are available. Ocean is a side lane for things that can only be obtained at sea. There is no separate "multiple" lane. Amber, Amber Pearl, Coins, Ruby and Silver Necklace sit in the Meadows, and their other chest locations are listed on the entry.

Killing a boss moves the progression on. Anything that appears only after a boss is killed belongs to the next biome. Greydwarf, Greydwarf Eye and Greydwarf Trophy stay in the Black Forest, because greydwarfs roam the Meadows only after Eikthyr. Boss drops stay with their boss.

Trader stock that unlocks after a boss is listed one biome later:

- Thunder Stone and Ymir Flesh: Swamp (after The Elder).
- Wider Pockets, Fragrant Bundle, Mountain Peak Pepper Powder, Toadstool, Scythe Handle: Plains (after Moder).
- Grasslands Herbalist Harvest: Mistlands (Yagluth).
- Deeper Pockets, Herbs of the Hidden Hills: Ashlands (The Queen).
- Fiery Spice Powder: Deep North (Fader).

Recipes that follow those ingredients move with them: Hearty Mountain Logger's Stew, Mead base: Berserkir, Berserkir Mead and the Scythe to the Plains; Plains Pie Picnic to the Mistlands; Mushrooms Galore to the Ashlands; Ashlands Gourmet Bowl to the Deep North. Egg stays in the Plains (Sealed Tower chest, and hens).

Hildir's camp is in the Meadows, but never on the starting island, so reaching her needs a boat (the Karve, Black Forest stage). She and the items she sells with no chest unlock are listed in the Black Forest: Headband, Simple Purple/Red Cap, Simple Undyed Dress/Tunic, Sparkler, Barber Kit and Iron Pit, plus Iron Fire Pit (built from Iron Pit). Items that unlock with her returned chests stay in that chest's biome. Ocean items count as needing a boat as well, so they are Black Forest stage or later.

Hildir's returned chests place their unlocks in the chest's biome: Brass Chest in the Smouldering Tomb (Black Forest), Silver Chest in the Howling Cavern (Mountain), Bronze Chest in the Sealed Tower (Plains).

### Listed earlier than the usual tier

These 17 entries are listed in an earlier biome because a chest, a spawn or a recipe makes them reachable there. Their summaries say "normally {usual}-tier" so the usual tier stays visible.

- Bronze Battle Idol and Bronze Protection Idol: Meadows, in Abandoned House, Abandoned Village, Draugr Village and Viking Graveyard chests (0.2% per roll). Usual tier: Black Forest.
- Iron Battle Idol and Iron Protection Idol: Black Forest, in Burial Chambers, Contested Tower, Greydwarf Ruins, Greydwarf Tower, Skeleton Tower, Smouldering Tomb and Troll Cave chests (0.2% per roll). Usual tier: Swamp.
- Silver Battle Idol and Silver Protection Idol: Swamp, in Sunken Crypts, Swamp Grave and Swamp Runestone Tower chests (0.1% per roll). Usual tier: Mountain.
- Black Metal Battle Idol and Black Metal Protection Idol: Mountain, in Abandoned Cabin, Frost Caves, Howling Cavern, Inverted Tower (Mountain) and Mountain Tower chests (0.2% per roll). Usual tier: Plains.
- Black Marble Battle Idol and Black Marble Protection Idol: Plains, in Fuling Outpost, Fuling Ruin, Fuling Village and Sealed Tower chests (0.3% per roll). Usual tier: Mistlands.
- Draugr and Draugr Elite: Meadows. They spawn in Meadows Draugr Villages, which are in the world from the start. Usual tier: Swamp.
- Entrails, Draugr Trophy and Draugr Elite Trophy: Meadows, dropped by Draugr and Draugr Elite. Usual tier: Swamp.
- Frostcore: Deep North. The earliest source is a Winding Tunnels pickable. It is used for the Frigid Kiln (10) and the Frost Foundry (10). It is not found in Mountain frost caves. Usual tier: Mountain.
- Frost Foundry: Deep North, built with 10 Frostcore. Usual tier: Mountain.

### Relations

Crafted and built are separate. Crafted means a recipe at a crafting station, or by hand. Built means placed with the Hammer from materials, with a station nearby. Stations and station upgrades are built. An upgrade links from the station it improves, rather than appearing as a source of that station. Workbench leads to Chopping Block (built nearby, and it upgrades the Workbench).

Also recorded, and shown in the item panel: upgrade costs (quality 2–4) for 201 items; alternative recipes (Bronze from 10 Copper + 5 Tin yields 5; smelting Scrap Bronze, Copper Scrap and Scrap Iron; the Iron Cooking Station); Forge of Potential idols; station fuel (Bone Fragments and Charred Bone for the Shield Generator, Coal for the Smelter); Ballista and Catapult ammo; planting; the Obliterator.

Two alternative inputs are text only, because a link would loop: Finewood to Coal, and Copper Scrap to Copper.

Coins list creature drops, chests in every biome, and the nine valuables any trader buys. Spending them links to Haldor, Hildir and the Bog Witch, each with prices and unlocks. Meat Pile and Coin Pile are not coin sources. Coin Pile is a build piece made from Coins. A troll carries 20–30 Coins.

Trader stock: Hildir (38 items; Black Forest stage, because her Meadows camp is on another island), Haldor (11 items, including Wider Pockets at 1,000 Coins and Deeper Pockets at 2,000), Bog Witch (20 items). Boss unlocks and Hildir's returned chests are unlock links.

Valuables, same price at every trader: Amber 5, Amber Pearl 10, Ancient Coin 10, Ruby 20, Silver Necklace 30, Grimvarn 55, Solryth 95, Veydris 135, Draumyx 175 Coins.

Yggdrasil Wood is cut with a Black Metal Axe or better. An earlier required tool, Jotun Bane, is itself made from Yggdrasil Wood.

### What the 28 Oct audit changed

All 1,068 entries then in the set were checked against valheim.source.gs (Valheim 1.0.20), the Jotunn datamine and the Fandom wiki, with valheim.tools used in the browser for the open points. The set grew to 1,098 entries: 29 missing "Cast: …" intermediates of the Deep North casting chain and the Birds' Nest were added, and 123 errors of lane, name, station, recipe, build, drop or missing source were fixed. Structures were added after that, which is the 1,167-entry set above.

A pass against valheim.tools item and creature pages applied 44 corrections on 39 entries and marked 111 further entries verified. Examples: Crow to the Black Forest (3 Feathers); Seagull later moved to the Meadows; Midsummer Crown and Pointy Hat are Workbench recipes (seasonal, Black Forest); asksvin bones from carrion at Morgen Holes became Asksvin Skeleton; Moose meat 4–6 and hide or sinew 2–3; Seal pelt or blubber 2–3; Captive Fuling drops Ancient Coin at 100%, 1–2; The Void drops only Ectoplasm; Pike also takes Cold Fishing Bait.

Checked on valheim.tools at the same time: Deer White is a rare passive Black Forest variant with 30 health, dropping 2 Deer Meat and a trophy at 100% (spawn location still unconfirmed). Ice Skates and Ice Shoes are found in the Deep North; the exact source is unknown. Midsummer Crown (event only) and Pointy Hat have recipes and upgrades. Bloodgold idols are rare Deep North chest loot. Seagull, Crow and Ash Crow drop 3 Feathers at 100%. A Leviathan gives 3–4 Chitin per barnacle. Yggdrasil Wood needs a Black Metal Axe or better. Club upgrades cost 5/10/20 Bone Fragments. Thungr Trophy comes from the Zil and Thungr fight. An earlier note that Frostcore came from Frost Core Hangers in Mountain frost caves does not match the current Deep North listing above.

Putrid Hole (Morgen holes) had been mapped to the Deep North instead of the Ashlands, and 7 dungeons were missing. That was fixed on 18 entries. Ashwood, Charred Bone and Molten Core are found in the Ashlands, not the Deep North.

Names: Mead Cauldron is listed as Mead Ketill, the in-game name. "Root (olend)" is the creature Root. The Kvastur trophy is listed as "Kvastur Trophy" so it stays distinct from the miniboss; in game the item is just "Kvastur". Fader Relic was merged with Kindled Ribs (the name from 1.0.15). Yagluth's offering is 5 Fuling Totems. Bronze Nails are crafted in batches of 20. Iron Nails are crafted in batches of 10.

Hildir's keys are omitted. They cannot be obtained in a normal game; Hildir unlocks her returned chests herself.

### Gear and creatures

110 creatures: regular creatures, animals, tameables, minibosses (Brenna, Geirrhafa, Zil, Thungr, Kvastur, Lord Reto) and Ocean (Serpent, Leviathan), each linked to its drops.

Forge of Potential for Nord weapons uses the Bloodgold Battle Idol, not a Black Metal idol. Nord Sword past quality 4 costs 1 idol, 45 Bloodgold and 22 Timberwood. Nord Sword is cast at Black Forge level 4 (20 Bloodgold, 10 Timberwood, Mould) and finished at the Frost Foundry; further upgrades are at Black Forge levels 5, 6 and 7. Upgrade costs for the other Nord gear are not recorded. The set includes the Nord weapons, shields, staves and armour (cast from a mould at the Frost Foundry) and the Frostfire and Thunderblood variants.

Ballista: Hammer near a Workbench, 10 Black Metal, 10 Yggdrasil Wood, 3 Mechanical Spring. Ammo: Wooden, Black Metal, Flametal and Bloodgold Missile. Catapult: 20 Ashwood, 10 Flametal, 1 Charred Cogwheel. Ammo: Grausten, Explosive and Bloodgold Payload. Each ammo sits in the biome of its latest ingredient or station.

Riktig Fuling, Larva and Blue Mushroom exist only through console commands. Blue Mushroom's pickable never generates. They stay in their nominal biome with no links, and their drops are omitted.

### Scope, and what is left out

The set started as 183 hand-picked entries and grew to every non-decorative row of the source list, plus functional stations, upgrades, ships, beds, chests and the three traders.

Left out: decorative build pieces, stacks, rugs and banners; places; and 29 internal, unused or unobtainable rows (for example Skelett, Stone Pickaxe, The Hive, Bonemaw Scale, Hooded Lantern, summoned creatures and Hildir's keys).

Still unknown: where Deer White spawns, the exact source of Ice Skates and Ice Shoes, and Nord Sword upgrade costs at Black Forge levels 5, 6 and 7. Fermenter yields are not shown.
